What kind of air pollution, where, and when

AQI says how bad the air is. This tracker shows what kind of pollution it is: winter smog, dust, everyday haze or clean air, found by clustering daily pollutant readings. It is meant to help pollution boards and city officials pick the right action for each city and season, and to help people know what they are breathing.

Result

Data

  • India Daily Weather and Air Quality (Kaggle): 50 cities, daily, 5 Aug 2022 to 30 Oct 2026. Pollutants are model estimates (Copernicus CAMS via Open-Meteo); weather is from Open-Meteo.
  • CPCB hourly station AQI (CPCB AQI repository, via GitHub Vonter/india-cpcb-aqi): used only to check the estimates. Monthly AQI correlation 0.50 across 40 cities. So use this tracker for patterns and types, not exact daily values.
  • India outline: datameet maps (india-composite).

Model

K-means, k = 4, on log scaled PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, O3, CO, dust and the PM2.5 to PM10 ratio for 77,400 city-days. k chosen with the elbow method, silhouette (0.23) and resampling stability (0.99). The official AQI category as a grouping scores 0.03. Weather was not used in the model; it is used to explain the types.

Limits

  • One point per city, not per ward or village.
  • Types describe the pollutant mix; they suggest a cause but do not prove it.
  • Not a health alert system; official alerts must come from CPCB stations.

Use of AI

Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) were used to find datasets, write and debug the Python analysis and this dashboard's code, and structure the presentation. Problem choice, features, k and type names were decided by the author; all numbers come from the notebook.
